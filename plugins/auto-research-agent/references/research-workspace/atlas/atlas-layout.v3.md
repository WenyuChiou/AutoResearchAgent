# Atlas selected relationship basis contract v3

This presentation revision extends the retained [v2 relation contract](atlas-layout.v2.md).
The v6 layout, selection-only graph edges and all preceding evidence requirements
remain applicable.

The textual classification and relationship basis is hidden without a valid typed
paper, direction or method selection. Clicking a node or its lower summary opens
only the basis incident to that exact identity. Default paper details and hover
do not select a graph node. Clearing focus hides the basis again. The planar
fallback follows the same rule; switching 2D/3D or showing names cannot broaden it.

Recorded topic and method memberships remain readable even for a single paper.
Recorded overlaps and optional computed similarity retain their separate labels;
missing classification remains missing. Original source text is displayed as text.
This is visibility over saved records, not a change to canonical data, research
scores, source acquisition, execution permissions or scientific quality.
