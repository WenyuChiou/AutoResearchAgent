## Design Context

### Users

Researchers and teammates compare supported directions, inspect data/method
conditions, save a preference or valid selection, and continue into planning.
They need decision-relevant summaries before complete evidence and receipts.

### Brand Personality

Clear, calm, research-focused. Retain the existing V9 research-workbench
palette and meaningful icons; avoid decorative redesign and dense technical UI.

### Aesthetic Direction

Readable working pages with deliberate whitespace, aligned blocks, restrained
navy/teal emphasis and consistent controls. Important information is visible;
secondary detail is available through clear disclosures.

### Design Principles

1. Summary and next action before optional detail.
2. Consistent panel gutters, type scale and readable line lengths everywhere.
3. One meaningful primary action; visible pending, error and saved states.
4. Adapt the layout for narrow screens without hiding essential information.
5. Verify actual pixels independently; preserve evidence, uncertainty and safety.

Detailed rules and measurable acceptance: [Wiki UI/UX principles](harness-ui/UI_UX_PRINCIPLES.md).
